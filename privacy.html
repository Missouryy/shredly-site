Overview

Shredly is designed around your own library. It does not require a Shredly account, and the app does not use advertising trackers or analytics services. iCloud sync is optional. A separate recap feature uses Apple's Private Cloud Compute only when you ask for a summary.

What stays on your device

When you import a file, Shredly saves a copy in its local library. The app also stores information needed for reading and continuity, such as extracted text and thumbnails, reading position, shredding status, goals, activity history, and preferences.

Text recognition for imported images and scanned pages uses Apple's device frameworks. Speech playback uses iOS speech features. Shredly does not send these files to a Shredly-operated server.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable sync in Settings, Shredly uses Apple's CloudKit service and the private database associated with your signed-in Apple Account. Synced data can include document files, reading progress and status, settings, and activity totals. Shredly uses the system iCloud account; it does not ask for your Apple Account password or receive your Apple Account email address from CloudKit.

Turning sync off stops future sync activity in the app. It does not automatically erase copies already stored on your device or in iCloud. Apple's iCloud privacy information explains how Apple handles iCloud data.

Yesterday's Reading

When you finish paragraphs, Shredly keeps their text locally for recent daily recaps. If you open Yesterday's Reading and request a summary, the completed excerpts needed for that summary are sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute language model. The result is cached on your device. This feature depends on device eligibility, Apple Intelligence availability, and iOS 27 or later. Recap excerpts are not part of Shredly's iCloud sync.

This website

This is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. Shredly does not add analytics scripts, ad trackers, forms, or its own cookies. The light or dark theme you select is saved in your browser's local storage. GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver the site under its privacy statement.

Following an external link is handled by that destination. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the message and sender address so we can reply.

Your choices

  • Use Shredly without enabling iCloud sync.
  • Turn sync on or off in the app's Settings.
  • Choose whether to use Yesterday's Reading.
  • Remove individual documents from your library. For help with iCloud copies or privacy questions, contact us.
