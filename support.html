How do I add a document?

Open Library, go to Browse, then use the import button to choose a file from Files. Your imported copy is saved in Shredly's local library.

Which files can I read?

Shredly supports TXT, Markdown, RTF, HTML, Pages, DOCX, EPUB, PDF, JPG, JPEG, and PNG. For images and scanned PDFs, readable text depends on text recognition quality.

Can I bring shredded text back?

Yes. Turn on Restore Shredded Text in Settings if you want to be able to revisit completed text. Your original imported file remains in the library until you delete it.

How does iCloud sync work?

Sync is optional. Sign in to iCloud on your device, then enable iCloud sync in Shredly Settings. Use the same Apple Account on your other device and tap Sync Now if you want to retry. If a sync fails, changes stay saved on the device.

Where is my reading activity?

Your daily goal and activity calendar are on Home. Shredly tracks reading time on your device; optional iCloud sync can carry activity history between devices.

What is Yesterday's Reading?

On supported devices with Apple Intelligence, you can open a recap of completed reading from the previous day. This feature uses Apple's Private Cloud Compute and requires iOS 27 or later. Read how it handles excerpts.
